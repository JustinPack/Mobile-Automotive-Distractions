# Mobile Automotive Distractions

Turn an unattended running vehicle into a moving distraction.

## Installation

Enable **ZombieBuddy** and **Mobile Automotive Distractions**, then restart the game. For multiplayer, the server and each player need ZombieBuddy and **Mobile Automotive Distractions (Multiplayer)**. ZombieBuddy may ask you to review the mod's Java component when it is first installed or updated.

## Rigging a vehicle

1. Enter a vehicle, start its engine normally, stop and get out. Stand by the driver's door and right-click the vehicle. Choose **Rig as distraction**.
2. Engage the parking brake if you want the vehicle to remain still during setup. You can configure the controls in any order, but wedging the accelerator while the brake is off immediately sends the vehicle away.
3. Drag a **stone** or **wrench** from your inventory onto the accelerator. In multiplayer, keep the wedge, rope, plank and duct tape in your main inventory. Choose a slow, medium or full target speed. Set the gear to forward or reverse.
4. Optionally attach a **rope** or a **plank** to secure the steering wheel. A plank also requires **duct tape**, which is consumed. Set the direction and, for a turn, its rate. Without a restraint the car initially travels straight before gradually turning. With the wheel locked straight, it travels farther before slight drift begins.
5. Optionally rig the horn. This requires a **screwdriver** and level 3 in **Electrical or Mechanics**. The screwdriver is not consumed. The horn begins sounding immediately.
6. Release the parking brake to send the vehicle away.

To remove a rig, wait until the vehicle is stopped, stand by the driver's door and right-click **Disarm distraction rig**, or use **Disarm and recover** during setup. The accelerator wedge and rope can be recovered; a plank has a 50% chance of recovery. Components cannot be recovered from a destroyed vehicle. A severe impact can knock the accelerator wedge loose, while the rigged horn continues until the battery runs out or the rig is disarmed.

The driver's seat is unavailable while a rig is attached. Other seats remain accessible.
